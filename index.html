Key NCERT Observation: Inside a current-carrying solenoid, field lines are parallel and uniform. Inserting a soft iron core multiplies field strength (B = μ₀ · μᵣ · n · I).

Clock Face Rule: Looking at an end of the coil, if current flows clockwise, that face acts as a South Pole; if anti-clockwise, it acts as a North Pole.
